#  HR Employee Attrition Analysis using SQL

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes the HR Employee Attrition dataset using SQL to answer important business questions related to employee turnover. SQL queries were used to extract insights, summarize employee information, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to use SQL for exploring the HR Employee Attrition dataset and identify key factors influencing employee attrition through business-focused queries.

---

##  Tools Used

- MySQL
- MySQL Workbench

---

##  Dataset

- HR Employee Attrition Dataset

---

##  Database

- Database Name: **HR_Employee_Attrition**

This project answers 25 business questions, including:

2. Employee attrition count and percentage.
3. Gender-wise employee distribution.
4. Department-wise employee count.
5. Job Role distribution.
6. Average monthly income.
7. Employees working overtime.
8. Attrition by department.
9. Attrition by gender.
10. Attrition by age group.
11. Average salary by education level.
12. Average salary by job role.
13. Employees with the highest monthly income.
14. Average years at the company.
15. Average distance from home.
16. Job satisfaction analysis.
17. Work-life balance analysis.
18. Attrition based on overtime.
19. Marital status distribution.
20. Business travel analysis.
21. Employees by education field.
22. Years since last promotion.
23. Average training times last year.
24. Employees with the highest experience.
25. Other HR business insights using SQL.

---

##  Key Insights

- Total Employees: **1,470**
- Employees Left: **237**
- Attrition Rate: **16.12%**
- Sales department recorded the highest attrition.
- Employees working overtime showed a higher attrition rate.
- Employees aged 26–35 experienced the highest attrition.
- Lower job satisfaction was associated with higher attrition.
- Employees with fewer years at the company were more likely to leave.

---

##  Business Recommendations

- Reduce employee overtime where possible.
- Improve work-life balance initiatives.
- Focus retention efforts on high-attrition departments.
- Review salary and promotion policies.
- Increase employee engagement and job satisfaction.
- Strengthen onboarding and career development programs.
