This folder contains the Python implementation of the HR Employee Attrition Analysis project, including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, and business insights.

##  Objectives

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing
- Conduct Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Answer business questions
- Visualize employee trends
- Generate business insights

##  Analysis Performed

### Data Cleaning

- Checked missing values
- Removed duplicates
- Verified data types
- Prepared dataset for analysis

### Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

- Employee distribution
- Department analysis
- Salary analysis
- Age distribution
- Attrition analysis

### Business Questions

1. How many employees left the company?
2. What is the employee attrition percentage?
3. Does lower salary increase employee attrition?
4. Are employees who work overtime more likely to leave?
5. Which department has the highest employee attrition?
6. Which age group has the highest employee attrition?
7. Do job satisfaction and work-life balance affect employee attrition?
8. Which education level has the highest salary?
9. Does distance from home influence employee attrition?
10. Do employees with fewer years at the company have higher attrition?

---

##  Key Insights

- Attrition Rate: **16.12%**
- Overtime employees were more likely to leave.
- Sales department had the highest attrition.
- Employees aged 26–33 years showed the highest attrition.
- Higher job satisfaction reduced attrition.
- Better work-life balance improved employee retention.
